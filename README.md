# 🪙 Metal & Tokenized Commodity Intelligence Agent (v1.3.0)

> **On-Chain Physical Assets (PAXG/XAUT), CoinGecko Metal Scanner & Global Commodity Supply Constraints Intelligence Engine with X402 Payment Retry Support.**

`Metal & Commodity Intelligence Agent` is an autonomous AI agent that analyzes on-chain mint/burn activity for tokenized gold and silver (PAXG, XAUT), real-time `gold-backed` and `commodity-backed` market data via CoinGecko API, COMEX gold/silver registered vault inventory trends, US national debt dynamics, and global mine production vs. central bank physical absorption rates. It operates in direct sequence with the **13-Chain** surveillance layer.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **Tokenized Metal On-Chain Surveillance:**
  * Monitors active mint and burn events for PAXG and XAUT on Ethereum.
  * Calculates the Gold/Silver Ratio and provides historical range analysis.
* **CoinGecko Metal & Gold Category Scanner:**
  * Automatically tracks token prices, 24-hour performance, and trading volume under `gold-backed` and `commodity-backed` categories.
  * Instantly detects precious metal token price movements (+5% or higher) and generates signals.
* **COMEX Vault & Central Bank Gold Analytics:**
  * Tracks physical metal withdrawal trends in COMEX (Registered Vaults).
  * Analyzes quarterly net central bank purchases, de-dollarization trades, and physical backing demands routed via the **X402 Protocol**.
* **Mine Supply Bottleneck Dynamic Engine:**
  * Computes dynamic market tightness based on central bank annual absorption (~1,000 tonnes) against global annual mine output (~3,600 tonnes / ~27.8% lockup).
  * Generates Critical Supply Crunch signals for physical spot markets.
* **US Debt Clock & Devaluation Index Integration:**
  * Fetches real-time US national debt ($39.9T+) and daily interest expense ($3.5B+) metrics to calculate the Paper-to-Silver Ratio and Fiat Devaluation Signals.
* **X402 / uAgents Retry Payment Protocol (NEW):**
  * Executes up to 3 asynchronous retry verifications (at 3-second intervals) upon receiving payment notifications (`CommitPayment`) from client agents.
  * Implements robust error handling (HTTP 402-style retry request notifications) to enhance commodity intelligence commerce.
* **uAgents Protocol Native Support:**
  * Receives `MetalDataQueryRequest`, computes dynamic quotes (0.2 ~ 0.5 FET) based on requested symbols (`PAXG`, `XAUT`, `GOLD`, `SILVER`, `ALL`), and executes autonomous data delivery.
